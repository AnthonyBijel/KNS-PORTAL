ID PROCESSING FOR OLD STUDENTS (THOSE WHO SHIFTED AND STILL HAVEN’T RECEIVED THEIR ID’S) To facilitate the processing of your official school ID, please accomplish the Google ID Processing Form through the link provided by the school. Important Reminders: Make sure that you really haven't received your I.D. yet or you shifted course before proceeding; 1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. 2. Use your correct and complete name as reflected in your enrollment records. 3. Upload a recent 1x1 ID picture with a white background and proper collar attire. 4. Ensure that the photo is clear, properly cropped, and follows the prescribed ID picture guidelines. 5. Rename your 1x1 ID picture document using your FULL NAME as file name. 6. Accepted 1x1 picture format are JPEG, PNG, and JPG. 7. Double-check all information before submitting the form. 8. Submit your response on or before SEPTEMBER 4, 2026 Your cooperation will help ensure the timely preparation and release of your school ID
